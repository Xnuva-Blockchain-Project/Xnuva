# Xnuva (XNUV) — transparency policy

The XNUVA Blockchain Project does not sell coins, conduct ICOs, presales or private coin sales, or solicit investment in these coins. Mining on the respective blockchain is the recommended method of acquisition. Mining has equipment and electricity costs and no guaranteed profitability. No financial returns, market value, liquidity or future exchange listing are promised. The project does not operate or promote an official exchange listing. Do not assume coins are readily tradeable.

Never pay someone claiming to sell coins on behalf of the project. Verify releases and protect private keys. Lack of an exchange listing is not proof of fraud or safety. Evaluate software, security, decentralisation and historical evidence independently.
